Add Habits to your home screen

  1. Tap Share (the square with the arrow) at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. If you see an Open as Web App switch, turn it off.
  4. Tap Add.

You'll only see this page once. The icon you add skips it and opens your habit app straight away, in Safari, where you're signed in to Claude.
